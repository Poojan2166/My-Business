Questions

Good to know.

We strongly recommend booking 3-5 days ahead for weekend slots and 2 weeks ahead for bridal consultations. Weekday afternoons often have same-day availability.

We use premium, certified products from leading global brands such as L’Oréal Professionnel, Schwarzkopf, Wella, and Olaplex. For skincare and makeup, we select dermatologist-tested organic lines and high-definition cosmetics.

Yes, we offer premium on-location packages for bridal parties and special events. Our senior styling team will travel to your venue or home, fully equipped to deliver the complete Maison Auré experience.

We kindly request at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling or cancelling individual appointments. Bridal and group packages require a 72-hour notice to avoid deposit forfeitures.
