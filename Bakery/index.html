/ Our Story

A tradition of slow baking.

"We opened in a single ground-floor room with one deck oven and a bag of flour from a neighbor's farm. Forty years on, the room is a little larger, the oven a little hotter, and the flour still comes from a friend."

Maison Brulé is a bakery that refuses shortcuts. Our levain is fed twice daily, our croissants laminated by hand, our coffee poured by people who can pronounce the farm it came from. We do not bake to fill shelves. We bake to mark the day.

We offer a selection of vegan pastries made with plant-based butter and milk. However, due to the high volume of wheat flour in our air, we cannot guarantee a gluten-free environment.
