A look inside

The room itself.

Open every detail to see what we mean by a room built for practice.

The Main Shala

Reclaimed ash floors, clay-plaster walls, and floor-to-ceiling windows that capture the soft morning light. No mirrors, no music, no clocks — just space for steady, focused practice.

Natural Materials

High-density cork mats and linen props, cleaned with organic lavender oil after every practice. Everything you need is laid out and waiting for you.

Unhurried Space

A room designed for twelve mats, never more. Spacious layouts ensure you have room to move, breathe, and receive individual guidance from teachers.

The Tea Nook

A quiet transition area. Sit before or after class with a cup of warm herbal tea, let your mind settle, and transition back into the world at your own pace.

Breath & Ventilation

A silent air-exchange system that filters the room four times an hour, bringing in fresh outside air and maintaining perfect humidity for practice.
