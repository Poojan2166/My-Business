About Helion

Forty-two years of
quiet, uncompromising
engineering.

Founded in 1983 in a single machine shop in Stuttgart, Helion Industries has grown into a global manufacturing partner for the companies redefining flight, mobility and energy. We don't chase trends — we compound decades of tolerance, discipline and craft.

Today, 12,400 engineers, machinists and quality specialists produce more than 340 million components a year, shipped to 62 countries and integrated into everything from commercial jetliners to lithography systems.

Zero-defect isn't a slogan.

Every process, every part, every batch — measured, logged and cryptographically signed. When your regulator asks, the answer takes 3 seconds, not 3 weeks.
