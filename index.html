The Academy

A conservatoire built for the craft of dance.

Auréa began as a single mirrored room and a promise: that a serious dancer deserves a serious home. Fourteen years on, we train across eight disciplines with faculty drawn from Kalakshetra, RAD and the world's studio floors.

Our Vision

To be South Asia's most respected private conservatoire for movement.

Our Mission

Nurture artists with technical rigor, emotional depth and quiet confidence.
