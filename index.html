About the group

Four decades of quiet excellence — engineered into every part we ship.

Founded in 1983 in a single machine shop, Nordforge today operates eleven vertically integrated facilities across four continents. We do one thing: build parts that cannot fail — and build them the same way, every time, whether it's ten pieces or ten million.

Core capabilities

Six disciplines.
One vertically
integrated shop floor.

From billet to boxed assembly, every step of production happens inside Nordforge — so quality, lead time and traceability never depend on someone else's schedule.

5-axis CNC machining

Sub-micron tolerances on Inconel, titanium and hardened tool steel. 340 spindles running 22/7.

Precision forging & casting

Investment casting to 12kg parts, closed-die forging up to 4,500 tons — metallurgically certified.

Electronics & PCBA

IPC-A-610 Class 3 assembly for avionics, EV and medical. In-house X-ray and AOI inspection.

Additive manufacturing

Metal DED and LPBF for low-volume complex geometries — flight-qualified for two Tier-1 primes.

System assembly

Cell-based assembly with digital work instructions, torque-verified, serialised end-to-end.

Metrology & test

Climate-controlled labs with CMM, laser scanning and destructive testing — ISO/IEC 17025 accredited.
