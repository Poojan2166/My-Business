/ABOUT POLYSTRAT

Three decades
of engineered
polymers.

Founded in 1996, Polystrat has grown from a single extrusion line into a globally distributed polymer manufacturing group. We engineer custom thermoplastic compounds and high-precision components for mission-critical industries.

Our 240,000 sq ft facility houses 80+ injection molding machines, in-house tooling, and a dedicated R&D laboratory. Every batch is traceable from raw resin to finished part.

/FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions answered.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product family. Standard compounds start at 500 kg; custom injection molded parts begin at 1,000 units for prototype runs, 10,000+ for production.

How quickly can you ship samples?

Standard sample plaques or resin pellets ship within 48 hours. Custom color matches or test runs typically require 5-7 business days.

Do you support custom formulations?

Yes, our R&D lab can formulate polymers with specific additives for UV resistance, flame retardancy, custom colors, or structural reinforcements (e.g., glass fiber).

What certifications do you hold?

We maintain ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485 for medical devices, IATF 16949 for automotive components, and ISO 14001 for environmental management systems.

Can you handle export logistics?

Yes, we ship to over 45 countries worldwide. We offer DDP, CIF, and FOB terms, with dedicated temperature-controlled cold-chain options for medical polymers.

What is your typical lead time?

Lead times range from 2 weeks for stock resin granules to 4-6 weeks for custom high-tolerance molded components.
