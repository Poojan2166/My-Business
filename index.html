Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying.

What is the delivery time?

Metro cities get same-day or next-day delivery. Rest of India ships in 2–4 working days, with free shipping on all prepaid orders.

Do you provide exchange offers?

Yes. Get an instant on-screen valuation for your old phone, free doorstep pickup, and the exchange value adjusted straight off your bill.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

COD is available on orders up to ₹50,000 across 19,000+ pin codes. Higher value orders need prepaid payment or EMI.

Are the products original?

Every device is 100% original, brand-authorised and sealed, and ships with a GST invoice plus full manufacturer warranty.

What is your return policy?

Easy 7-day returns on unused products in original packaging. Pickup is free and refunds are processed within 4–5 working days.

How does EMI work?

Choose No-Cost EMI at checkout on credit or debit cards from all major banks, with tenures from 3 to 24 months and instant approval.
