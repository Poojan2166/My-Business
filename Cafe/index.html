N° 002 — our small philosophy

We do four things, and we do them slowly.

01

Source, with patience

We taste two hundred green samples a year. We buy from twelve farms, three of which we have visited in person, and one we now call by first name.

02

Roast, with restraint

Our roaster is a 1956 Probat, refurbished. We roast in small batches on Tuesdays only, and rest every bean for a full week before it touches a grinder.

03

Brew, with attention

Every coffee on our bar is timed, weighed, and tasted before it leaves the counter. If it is not right, we pull it again, no questions.

04

Serve, with grace

No upsell, no flavored syrup, no shouting over the music. A flat white tastes better when you are not asked about the size.

N° 005 — a day, in five rituals

The shape of a weekday.

We open early and close gently. Here is what an average day looks like, from the first grind to the last cup.

06.30

First pull

Doors not yet open. The grinder warms, the espresso machine settles into temperature, the chalkboard gets today's bean.

07.00

Doors open

Regulars first. Their orders are already on the rail when they walk in.

10.45

The pastry cutoff

Whatever Aleyamma sent over is gone by now. We refuse to fake it with anything frozen.

15.00

The afternoon cupping

Public cupping at the back table on Saturdays. Five coffees, no spoons spared.

19.30

The grinders rest

Last filter of the day. The room slows. Conversations go longer than the coffee.

N° 011 — a few small things

Questions we hear, most often.

01 Do you take reservations for the morning?

We operate on a walk-in basis for morning service. Just drop by and we'll find you a spot as quickly as possible.

02 Do you have plant milks?

Yes, we offer organic oat milk and house-made almond milk as alternatives for all of our espresso drinks.

03 Do you serve decaf?

We do. We serve a clean, water-processed decaf single-origin coffee that tastes just as good as our regular offerings.

04 Can I work from a laptop?

We welcome laptop users in designated window seating. We offer free high-speed Wi-Fi to keep you connected.

05 Where can I buy your beans?

You can purchase fresh whole-bean coffee directly at our counter or through our online store. We roast weekly.
