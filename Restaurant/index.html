Our kitchen

We cook in copper because it tells the truth about heat.

Maison Cuivre opened in a former metalworks at the edge of the old quarter. We kept the chimneys, the wide timber floors, and the long communal table where the smiths once shared lunch.

The menu changes every fortnight, written by hand and shaped by what arrived that morning — never more than eight courses, never the same twice in a season.

Before you arrive

A few small things.

Reservations open on the first day of each month at 10:00 AM local time for the following month. We recommend booking well in advance as tables fill quickly.

Yes, we accommodate most common allergies and dietary preferences (vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten-free) with at least 48 hours' notice. Please note your requirements during booking.

We suggest smart casual attire. While jackets are not required, we ask that guests refrain from wearing athletic wear, shorts, or flip-flops.

You may bring up to two bottles of wine per table that are not already present on our wine list. A corkage fee of 75€ per bottle will apply.

Yes, our main dining room, private events space, and facilities are fully wheelchair accessible. Please let us know if you require any specific assistance.
